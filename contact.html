Our Office

Lincoln Old Town Hall, 1848.

We are based at the Lincoln Old Town Hall — a 19th-century New England civic building at 25 Lincoln Road in Lincoln, Massachusetts. Built in 1848, it was dedicated from its first town meeting to "Political, Temperance, Antislavery & Peace Meetings." We are glad to work from a building with that kind of history.
