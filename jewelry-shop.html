Jewelry Shop — Hero Photograph
Project Details
  • LocationWellesley, MA
  • StatusUnder Administration
  • TypeCommercial Adaptive Reuse
  • ProgramGym converted to design studio / jewelry shop

A gym transformed into a space for making.

The Jewelry Shop project converts a former gymnasium in Wellesley into a refined design studio and retail space. The adaptive reuse preserves the generous volume of the original space while introducing new material layers that create an intimate, craft-focused environment.

The project is currently under administration.
